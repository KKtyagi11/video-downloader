About “Cookies from”

Some posts are only served to a signed-in account — private X posts, most of Instagram, age-restricted YouTube. Picking a browser lets the downloader borrow that browser's existing session for the request.

  • Cookies are read from the browser profile only while a download runs, kept in memory, and sent solely to the site being downloaded from.
  • They are never written to disk by this app and never leave your machine by any other route.
  • Saved settings contain the browser's name only — e.g. "browser": "chrome".
  • This window's own cookie store is emptied at every launch and blocked from being written to.
  • If you share this app with someone, it carries none of your data — settings live outside the build and are created fresh per machine.

Leave it on “No sign-in” unless a download actually fails asking for a login. Close the chosen browser first — Chrome and Edge lock their cookie database while running.
